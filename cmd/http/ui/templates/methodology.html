📖 Census Methodology & Metrics

Detailed breakdown of how the FFXIV Census collects data, determines active player populations, tracks new character creation, and measures storyline progression.

🔍 Data Collection & Ingestion

The FFXIV Census operates by indexing public character profiles from the official Square Enix Final Fantasy XIV Lodestone service and community APIs like Tomestone.

Character discovery runs via distributed, rate-limited background workers designed to scan ID sequences, detect new player registrations, and periodically refresh existing character profiles. All requests adhere to strict rate limiting and polite backoff policies to avoid impacting official infrastructure.

Profile Visibility: Players can restrict access to their Lodestone data through Square Enix account settings. The entire character profile page, achievements, login activity, minions, mounts, and other categories can each be set to private independently. When a character's profile page is hidden, the census cannot retrieve any data for that character. When only achievements are private, the character's basic data (name, world, race, job levels) is still collected, but achievement-dependent metrics such as active status and MSQ completion will be incomplete. Characters with fully private profiles are excluded from all census metrics.

⚡ Active Player Criteria

Because Square Enix does not publish real-time login statistics or active subscription counts, the census estimates activity from tracked progression milestones observed within a rolling 30-day activity window.

A character is designated as Active if their latest observed tracked milestone was earned within the last 30 days. This is a conservative progression signal, not the character's globally latest achievement or a direct login measurement.

Completing a tracked milestone confirms the character was played within the window while keeping Lodestone traffic bounded to the small ordered milestone chain.

Characters may still be playing after their latest tracked milestone. They are categorized as inactive when that milestone is older than 30 days or their Lodestone achievements are private, so this metric intentionally undercounts activity rather than inferring unobserved play.

🐥 New Characters Created (Chocobo Milestone)

In previous iterations, "New Characters" were estimated based on the date a character ID was first crawled. However, discovery timestamps can be misleading when older or returning characters are indexed during routine sweeps.

To guarantee true accuracy, the New Characters Created (Last 30 days) metric calculates new characters strictly by using the timestamp they achieved the early-game Chocobo milestone:

🏅
Achievement ID 590: "My Little Chocobo"
Grand Company Questline • Level 20 MSQ Milestone

Unlocked upon acquiring a company chocobo issued by the player's chosen Grand Company (Maelstrom, Order of the Twin Adder, or Immortal Flames) early in the Main Scenario Questline.

Strict Inclusion: Characters who have not earned this milestone (or whose Lodestone achievements remain private) are excluded from the New Characters timeline. This eliminates indexing artifacts and ensures that every counted point represents an authentic player who progressed past character creation into Eorzea.
